Dataset convention
------------------
- 10 firms x 20 sequential teaching-period labels = 200 level observations.
- Original Grunfeld years 1935-1954 are relabelled 2006-2025 only for teaching.
- invest  = real gross investment.
- value   = real market value of the firm.
- capital = real stock of plant and equipment.
- Raw monetary variables are in millions of 1947 dollars.
- First log differences leave 190 firm-period observations over 19 teaching-period positions.
- Teaching label 2020 corresponds to original Grunfeld year 1949.
- Teaching label 2012 corresponds to original Grunfeld year 1941.
- No modern event is inferred from a relabelled teaching-period position.

EViews documentation verification
---------------------------------
Current official EViews help was checked for:

1. Equation::wald
   - Joint coefficient restrictions are separated by commas.
   - The panel interaction test therefore uses:
       eq_break2020.wald c(4)=0, c(5)=0

2. Panel least squares
   - per=f requests period fixed effects.
   - cov=percluster requests period-clustered covariance.

3. Equation::chow
   - Chow breakpoint tests are documented for least-squares equations with
     breakpoint observations specified by dates/observation numbers.

4. Equation::breakls / Equation::breakspec
   - breakls estimates least-squares equations with Bai-Perron-type breakpoints.
   - method=glob, select=high, trim=15 is documented syntax.
   - breakspec displays the resulting breakpoint specification.

5. Equation::rls
   - Recursive least-squares views are documented only for non-panel OLS
     equations without ARMA terms.

6. Equation::multibreak
   - Multiple-breakpoint testing is documented only for non-panel list-specified
     OLS equations.

Therefore standard single-series Chow/recursive/multiple-break commands are not
applied blindly to a firm-major stacked panel. Common panel break positions are
represented explicitly using teaching-period interactions.
