==========================================================

Dataset convention
------------------
- 10 firms x 20 sequential teaching-period labels = 200 level observations.
- Original Grunfeld years 1935-1954 are sequentially relabelled 2006-2025.
- The relabelled values are instructional identifiers only, not modern calendar observations.
- invest  = real gross investment.
- value   = real market value of the firm.
- capital = real stock of plant and equipment.
- Raw monetary variables are measured in millions of 1947 dollars.
- ginvest, gvalue, and gcapital are first log differences and are dimensionless growth measures.

Transformation/sample accounting
--------------------------------
Level observations: 200
First-log-difference observations: 190
Usable PVAR(1) observations after one additional lag: 180
Usable PVAR teaching periods: 18 (labels 2008-2025; original periods 1937-1954)

Stationarity/cointegration qualification
----------------------------------------
Chapter 4 found heterogeneous/specification-sensitive evidence in logged levels and much
stronger stationarity evidence in first log differences. Chapter 17 treated cointegration as
a conditional robustness branch rather than an established mandatory long-run relation.
Chapter 19 therefore uses ginvest, gvalue, and gcapital for its principal predictive analysis.

Synchronous circular-block residual-bootstrap sensitivity
---------------------------------------------------------
Independent replication used the K=1 restricted firm-specific autoregression under each null,
resampled aligned residual rows synchronously across all 10 firms in circular time blocks of
length 2, generated the dependent variable recursively, held the proposed predictor path fixed,
and recomputed the DH statistic. B=1000 and reproducibility seed=1907. Adjusted upper-tail
probability = (1 + exceedances)/(B + 1).

Predictor                 Predicted                block-2 bootstrap p
investment growth         firm-value growth        0.351648
investment growth         capital-stock growth     0.000999
firm-value growth         investment growth        0.729271
firm-value growth         capital-stock growth     0.010989
capital-stock growth      investment growth        0.016983
capital-stock growth      firm-value growth        0.082917

These bootstrap values are independently programmed sensitivities; EViews Group::cause does not
produce this bootstrap automatically.

Dynamic stability
-----------------
Eigenvalues of A1:
  0.032919 + 0.264045 i
  0.032919 - 0.264045 i
 -0.196124

Moduli:
  0.266089, 0.266089, 0.196124

All are inside the unit circle, so the fitted reduced-form PVAR(1) is dynamically stable.
Stability does not validate the estimator or identify structural shocks.

Current EViews documentation verification
-----------------------------------------
Current official EViews documentation was checked for:

1. Group::cause
   - Syntax: group_name.cause(n, options)
   - `dh` requests the Dumitrescu-Hurlin panel test.
   - `p` prints output.
   - Example in current help: macro.cause(12, p, dh).

2. Panel causality testing
   - EViews distinguishes stacked homogeneous panel Granger tests from the heterogeneous
     Dumitrescu-Hurlin procedure.
   - The DH test is based on cross-section-specific bivariate regressions and averaged statistics.

3. Panel equation LS
   - `cx=f` specifies cross-section fixed effects.
   - `per=f` specifies period fixed effects.
   - `cov=percluster` specifies period clustering.

4. Structural panel VAR tooling
   - EViews currently lists a separately installed `PSVAR` add-in for a Pedroni panel structural
     VAR implementation.
   - This add-in is not the same estimator as the descriptive equation-by-equation two-way FE
     PVAR used in Chapter 19.
   - Ordinary time-series VAR/SVAR views apply to correctly constructed time-series VAR objects,
     not to a blindly stacked panel whose lags can cross firm boundaries.
